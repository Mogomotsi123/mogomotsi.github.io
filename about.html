Our Purpose What You Learn Our Process
01 • OUR PURPOSE

Learn before you risk.

Trading can be difficult to understand when you are starting out. TradeFlow Academy brings basic trading concepts together in one simple place.

Education comes first.

The goal of TradeFlow Academy is to help beginners understand the basics of the forex market and develop a more structured way of studying charts.

Instead of focusing only on finding trades, the focus is on learning how to analyse, plan, manage risk and review decisions.

The TradeFlow idea

Learn the market. Build a process. Practise consistently. Review your decisions.

Small improvements can come from understanding what you did and why you did it.

02 • WHAT YOU CAN LEARN

Build your trading foundation.

TradeFlow Academy focuses on simple concepts that can help you develop your understanding of the market.

01

Market Structure

Learn how traders study highs, lows and price movement to understand market structure.

02

Trade Planning

Learn how to think about entries, stop losses, targets and conditions before entering.

03

Risk Management

Understand why protecting your trading account is an important part of the trading process.

03 • THE TRADEFLOW APPROACH

Read. Plan. Manage. Review.

A structured process can help you become more consistent in how you study and record your trades.

01

Read

Study the market and understand what price is doing.

02

Plan

Decide what you are looking for before entering.

03

Manage

Control your risk and follow the plan you created.

04

Record

Write down what happened so you can study the trade.

05

Review

Look back at your decisions and identify what you can improve.

04 • CORE VALUES

The habits behind the process.

Learning to trade is not only about charts. It also involves patience, discipline and honest review.

01

Patience

Wait for your conditions instead of forcing a trade.

02

Discipline

Follow your process and respect the rules you create.

03

Risk Awareness

Understand that every trade carries risk and losses are possible.

04

Review

Use your journal to learn from both good and bad trades.

Learn. Practise. Review. Improve.

TradeFlow Academy is built around the idea that understanding the process matters. Keep learning, practise what you learn and use every experience as an opportunity to improve.
