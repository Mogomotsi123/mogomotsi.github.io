ABOUT TRADEFLOW

Learn.
Build your process.

A simple trading learning hub created to help traders understand the market and develop better habits.

OUR PURPOSE

Trading is a process.

TradeFlow Academy focuses on simple trading education, planning and risk management.

The goal is to help traders understand what they are looking at before making a trading decision.

WHAT YOU CAN LEARN

Build better habits.

01

Market Structure

Learn how price moves and how to identify important highs, lows and trends.

02

Trade Planning

Build a clear plan before entering a trade instead of making random decisions.

03

Risk Management

Understand stop losses, position size and the importance of protecting your account.

OUR APPROACH

Learn from every trade.

Winning trades and losing trades can both provide useful information.

Use the journal to record your trades, review your decisions and improve your process over time.

IMPORTANT

Education first.
