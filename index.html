01 — MULTI-TIMEFRAME ANALYSIS

Give every timeframe a job.

Don't use every chart for the same purpose. Start broad, then move down only when the bigger picture makes sense.

D1

Daily — Trend & context

Ask: Is the market generally making higher highs/higher lows or lower highs/lower lows? Mark major support, resistance and important zones.

  • Overall direction
  • Major structure
  • Key levels
H4

4 Hour — Zones & structure

Use H4 to study the areas where price may react. Look for supply, demand, breaks of structure and strong displacement.

  • Supply & demand
  • Structure changes
  • Reaction areas
M15

15 Minute — Entries

After price reaches your planned H4 area, use M15 for confirmation and a more precise entry. No setup = no trade.

  • Entry confirmation
  • Rejection / break
  • Defined stop
D1 trend → H4 zone → M15 confirmation → Entry + risk plan

02 — PRICE ACTION

A simple supply & demand workflow.

01

Find the context

Start on D1. Identify the broad market structure and major levels. Avoid deciding an entry from M15 alone.

02

Mark the H4 area

Look for a clear supply or demand area associated with a strong move away. Treat the zone as an area, not an exact magic price.

03

Wait for price to arrive

Patience matters. Price can touch a zone and continue straight through it. A zone by itself is not confirmation.

04

Confirm on M15

Look for your predefined confirmation, such as a rejection and structure break. Only enter when your complete checklist is satisfied.

05

Plan before clicking

Know the entry, invalidation level, stop loss, target and maximum amount you are willing to lose before placing the trade.

03 — RISK MANAGEMENT

Protect the account first.

A strategy can have losing trades. Risk management keeps one trade from becoming a disaster.

1

Define risk

Choose a maximum risk per trade before entering. Beginners should keep risk small and consistent while learning.

2

Use a stop loss

Place the stop where the setup is invalidated, not simply where the loss feels comfortable.

3

Respect position size

Lot size should come from your account size, chosen risk and stop distance—not from how confident you feel.

4

Avoid revenge trading

A loss is information, not a reason to immediately increase your lot size or chase the market.

06 — CORE IDEAS

Keep it simple.

Trend

Direction and structure on a higher timeframe.

Supply

An area where strong selling previously moved price away.

Demand

An area where strong buying previously moved price away.

Confirmation

A predefined price-action condition that supports your planned entry.
