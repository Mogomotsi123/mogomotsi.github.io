01 • MARKET STRUCTURE

Learn to read the market

Market structure helps you understand how price is moving. Start by identifying higher highs, higher lows, lower highs and lower lows.

HH

Higher High

Price moves above a previous high. This can show that buyers are pushing price higher.

HL

Higher Low

Price pulls back but stays above the previous low. This can support a bullish structure.

LH

Lower High

Price moves up but fails to reach the previous high. This can appear in a bearish structure.

LL

Lower Low

Price moves below a previous low. This can show that sellers are pushing price lower.

03 • TRADE PLANNING

Think before you enter

A setup is more than simply seeing price move. Build a plan before entering a trade.

01

Analyse

Look at the higher timeframe and identify the overall market structure.

02

Find Your Area

Look for important areas such as supply, demand or previous market structure.

03

Wait for Confirmation

Do not rush into a trade. Wait for your planned conditions to appear.

04

Set Your Risk

Know where your stop loss will be and how much you are prepared to risk before entering.

05

Plan the Exit

Decide where you would take profit or exit if the setup no longer matches your plan.

06

Record the Trade

Use your trading journal to record what happened and what you learned.
