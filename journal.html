Trading Journal

A trading journal helps you record your trades, understand your decisions and learn from both successful and unsuccessful trades.

📝 What to Record

  • Date and time of the trade
  • Currency pair
  • Timeframe used
  • Entry and exit price
  • Stop loss and take profit
  • Reason for entering the trade

📊 Review Your Trades

After a trade, look back at what happened. Ask yourself what you did correctly and what you could improve.

The purpose is not to blame yourself. It is to learn from your decisions and build better habits.

🧠 Trading Psychology

Record how you felt before and during the trade. Fear, greed, impatience and overconfidence can affect trading decisions.

🛡️ Risk Management

Record how much you risked on each trade. A journal can help you see whether you are following your own risk rules consistently.

💡 TradeFlow Tip

Don't use your journal only when a trade goes well. Your losing trades can also teach you important lessons.
