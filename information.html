TRADING INFORMATION

Understand the market.
Build your process.

Simple explanations of the main concepts used in technical analysis and trading.

01 — MARKET STRUCTURE

Read the market first.

Market structure helps you understand the direction and behaviour of price.

HH

Higher High

Price makes a high above the previous high. This can be part of an upward structure.

HL

Higher Low

Price makes a low above the previous low. It can support an upward structure.

LH

Lower High

Price makes a high below the previous high. It can be part of a downward structure.

02 — TIMEFRAMES

Give every timeframe a job.

D1

Daily

Use the Daily chart to understand the bigger picture, overall direction and major levels.

H4

4 Hour

Use H4 to study important supply and demand areas and changes in market structure.

M15

15 Minute

Use M15 for entry confirmation after price reaches a planned area.

03 — SUPPLY & DEMAND

Important reaction areas.

S

Supply

An area where strong selling previously pushed price away.

D

Demand

An area where strong buying previously pushed price away.

04 — RISK

Protect your account.

No strategy wins every trade. A trading plan should include a defined stop loss, position size and maximum acceptable risk.
