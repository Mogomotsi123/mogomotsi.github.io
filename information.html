01 — MARKET STRUCTURE

Understand price movement.

Market structure helps traders study how price is moving and identify possible changes in direction. Understanding highs and lows gives you a foundation for reading a chart.

HH

Higher High

Price makes a high above the previous high. This can be part of an upward market structure.

HL

Higher Low

Price pulls back but stays above the previous low. This can show continued upward structure.

LH

Lower High

Price makes a high below the previous high. This can be part of a downward structure.

LL

Lower Low

Price makes a low below the previous low. This can show continued downward movement.

BOS

Break of Structure

Price breaks an important previous high or low. Traders can study this for possible continuation or change.

CH

Change in Structure

A change in the way price forms highs and lows can suggest that the current movement may be changing.

02 — TIMEFRAMES

Give each timeframe a job.

Looking at different timeframes can help you understand both the bigger picture and possible entry areas.

D1

Daily

Study the bigger market picture, major trends and important levels.

H4

4 Hour

Study market structure and important areas inside the bigger picture.

M15

15 Minute

Look for possible entries, confirmations and smaller price movements.

03 — SUPPLY & DEMAND

Study important price areas.

Supply and demand areas can help traders identify places where strong buying or selling previously occurred.

D

Demand

A demand area is a price region where buying pressure previously pushed price higher.

S

Supply

A supply area is a price region where selling pressure previously pushed price lower.

ZONE

Mark the Area

Focus on the important price area rather than trying to predict the exact pip where price will react.

04 — RISK MANAGEMENT

Protect your trading account.

A trading strategy is only part of the process. Risk management helps control how much you can lose when a trade does not work.

01

Know Your Risk

Decide how much you are willing to risk before entering the trade.

02

Use a Stop Loss

A stop loss can help limit the loss if price moves against your trade.

03

Respect Your Plan

Avoid increasing your risk just because a trade is moving against you.

05 — TRADING PSYCHOLOGY

Control the process, not the market.

The market cannot be controlled. Your decisions, preparation and discipline can be controlled.

01

Patience

Wait for your setup instead of entering because you are afraid of missing a move.

02

Discipline

Follow your trading rules even when emotions make you want to change them.

03

Accept Losses

A losing trade is part of trading. Focus on following your process instead of chasing losses.

06 — THE TRADE PROCESS

Build a repeatable routine.

A simple process can help you make decisions consistently instead of trading randomly.

01

Analyse

Start with the bigger timeframe and understand the current market structure.

02

Mark

Mark important levels, supply and demand areas and possible zones of interest.

03

Confirm

Wait for your entry conditions instead of entering simply because price reaches an area.

04

Manage

Set your stop loss, target and risk before taking the trade.

05

Record

Write down what happened in your trading journal. Record both wins and losses.

06

Review

Look back at your trades and identify what you can improve on your next setup.

07 — BEFORE YOU TRADE

Ask yourself these questions.

A quick checklist can help you slow down and think before placing a trade.

✓

What is the market structure?

Is price making higher highs and higher lows, or lower highs and lower lows?

✓

Where is my area?

Have I identified an important level or supply and demand area?

✓

What is my risk?

Do I know where my stop loss is and how much I am risking before entering?

✓

Is there confirmation?

Am I following my setup, or am I entering because of emotion?
